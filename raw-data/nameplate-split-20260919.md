## S7 (GAC 传祺向往S7) — checked 2026-09-19: PHEV, stays P
Autohome/Sohu launch coverage call it a 中大型插混 SUV (16.98万 start); the maker's page is "2027款传祺向往S7 PHEV". It offers switchable plug-in-hybrid / range-extender *operating modes* on one hardware set, which is why some coverage says 增程. DB trims agree: 4 PHEV trims, 1.5L, `is_range_extender: false`, EV range 115/180/205 km. Not REEV; no scope action. Caveat for known-issues review: items may describe it in "range-extender mode" wording.
